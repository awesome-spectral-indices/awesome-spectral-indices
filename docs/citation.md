# How to cite

If you use the catalogue or any of its APIs in research, software, teaching, or operational work, please cite the relevant publications:

## Catalogue and Python API: `spyndex`

::: code-group

```bibtex
@article{montero2023standardized,
  title = {A standardized catalogue of spectral indices to advance the use of remote sensing in Earth system research},
  volume = {10},
  ISSN = {2052-4463},
  url = {http://dx.doi.org/10.1038/s41597-023-02096-0},
  DOI = {10.1038/s41597-023-02096-0},
  number = {1},
  journal = {Scientific Data},
  publisher = {Springer Science and Business Media LLC},
  author = {Montero,  David and Aybar,  César and Mahecha,  Miguel D. and Martinuzzi,  Francesco and S\"{o}chting,  Maximilian and Wieneke,  Sebastian},
  year = {2023},
  month = Apr 
}
```

```[APA]
Montero, D., Aybar, C., Mahecha, M. D., Martinuzzi, F., Söchting, M., & Wieneke, S. (2023). A standardized catalogue of spectral indices to advance the use of remote sensing in Earth system research. Scientific Data, 10(1). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-023-02096-0
```
