## Formula

```
R675/(R650*R700)
```

### Classification

- Application domain: `Vegetation`
- Sensing modalities: `Hyperspectral`

### Bands

- `R675`: Reflectance at 675 nm.
- `R650`: Reflectance at 650 nm.
- `R700`: Reflectance at 700 nm.
