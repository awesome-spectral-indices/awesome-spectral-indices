## Formula

```
(R800-R470)/(R800+R470)
```

### Classification

- Application domain: `Vegetation`
- Sensing modalities: `Hyperspectral`

### Bands

- `R800`: Reflectance at 800 nm.
- `R470`: Reflectance at 470 nm.

### Polarizations

No radar polarizations are used in this index.

No constants are used in this index.
